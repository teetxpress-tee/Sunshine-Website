Questions

Enquiry questions

How do I get a quotation?

Send us the site location, what you need made or cleaned, and your deadline. For signage and film we normally arrange a site survey before quoting, so the figure you get covers fabrication and installation rather than the panel alone.

What information should I send with my enquiry?

The more of these you can give, the faster we can come back to you: the site address, the type of work (signboard, road sign, wallpaper or film, vehicle wrap, facade cleaning), rough size or area, whether it needs to be illuminated, and the date you need it finished.

How quickly will you reply?

WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach the team during working hours. Email enquiries are answered in the order they arrive, so include your phone number if the job is urgent.

Do you charge for a site survey?

Tell us where the site is when you enquire and we will confirm the arrangement before anyone comes out, so there are no surprises.

Can you work outside Selangor?

Yes. We are based in Petaling Jaya and install across the Klang Valley, and we have worked further afield including projects in Melaka, Johor and Pahang.
