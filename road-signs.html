Questions

Road signage questions

Do you make road signs and traffic signage?

Yes. We fabricate reflective statutory road signage including stop (BERHENTI) signs, chevron hazard markers, directional and warning boards, plus car park and internal traffic signage for private developments.

What material are road signs made from?

Typically aluminium plate with retro-reflective sheeting so the sign returns headlight glare at night. The reflective grade is chosen for the road type and speed.

Can you supply signage for a private road or car park?

Yes. Car parks, industrial estates, private roads and housing developments all need directional, speed and warning signage, and we fabricate and install to the layout you provide.

Do you install the posts and footings too?

Yes. Our crews handle post setting, footings and mounting, so the sign arrives ready to stand rather than as a loose panel.
