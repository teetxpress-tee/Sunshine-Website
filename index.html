Questions

Common questions from buyers

What does Sunshine Advertising & Media do?

Sunshine Advertising & Media Sdn. Bhd. is a signage, printing and branding fit-out contractor based in Petaling Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia. We design, print, fabricate and install illuminated signage, shopfront and monument signs, vehicle wraps, large-format print and complete retail and office branding fit-outs.

Where is Sunshine Advertising & Media located?

Our office and workshop are at W103, West Wing Metropolitan Square, Jalan PJU 8/1, Damansara Perdana, 47820 Petaling Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia. We install across the Klang Valley and nationwide.

What types of signage do you make?

We fabricate illuminated and LED channel letters, halo-lit lettering, LED neon, monument and township entrance signs, shopfront fascias, lightboxes, projecting signs, glass frosting and wayfinding, plus reflective statutory road signage.

Do you install the signage as well as make it?

Yes. We handle the full chain in-house: site survey, artwork and production drawings, printing and fabrication, then installation with our own crews. One contractor owns the job from brief to final bolt.

Who are some of your clients?

Our portfolio includes work for property developers such as Gamuda and IJM Land, and retail and service brands including Grab, KKV, Kodak City Lens, llaollao, Joymom's and True Life Sciences.

How do I request a quotation?

Call +6018-355 9191 or email contact@sunshinemedia.com.my with your site location, the type of sign or wrap you need, and your deadline. We will arrange a site survey before quoting.
