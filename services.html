Our Services

Everything we make,
install and clean

One contractor for signage, road signs, wallpaper and film, vehicle wraps, facade cleaning and renovation — printed, built and installed in-house.

Most jobs we take involve more than one of these. A store opening needs the unit built, the fascia fabricated, the glass filmed and the window dressed — so we kept all of it under one roof rather than sub-letting it out. Below is the full list of what we do.

01

Signage & Signboards

Designed, fabricated in our workshop and installed by our own crews — from a single shoplot fascia to a township monument sign.

  • LED front-lit channel letters
  • Halo-lit / backlit lettering
  • Monument & township entrance signs
  • Shopfront fascia signboards
  • Lightboxes & projecting signs
  • Acrylic & stainless fabricated letters
  • ACP panel signs with UV print
  • Ceremonial & feature arches
  • Sign servicing, cleaning & refacing
See the work →
02

Road Signs & Traffic Signage

Reflective aluminium signage built on steel frames, installed with posts and footings, crane and boom-lift work included.

  • Overhead highway gantry boards
  • Exit, destination & distance signs
  • Statutory road signs (BERHENTI, give way, speed)
  • Chevron & hazard markers
  • Directional & estate wayfinding
  • Car park & private road signage
  • Posts, concrete footings & installation
See the work →
03

Wallpaper & Film

Wall and glass surfaces re-finished without stripping anything out — supplied and installed.

  • Custom-printed wallpaper & wallcovering
  • Interior woodgrain / architectural wrapping film
  • Frosted & sandblast-effect glass film
  • Cut vinyl patterns & glass branding
  • Smart film (PDLC switchable privacy glass)
  • Solar film — heat, glare & UV rejection
  • One-way vision film
  • Printed shopfront window graphics
See the work →
04

Vehicle Wrap & Fleet Livery

Printed on cast vinyl with UV laminate and fitted around handles, seams and curves so the graphics stay put.

  • Full vehicle wrap / colour change
  • Partial wrap, decals & contact details
  • Lorry & box-body livery
  • Refrigerated truck wraps
  • Illuminated car-top displays
  • Fleet branding programmes
See the work →

Facade & ACP Cleaning

Restoring a frontage dulled by rain streaking, exhaust film and algae — cleaned to a method matched to the panel coating.

  • ACP panel cleaning & finish restoration
  • Glass panel & curtain wall washing
  • Facade & cladding wash
  • Signage cleaning & refacing
  • Post-construction cleaning
  • Rope access for multi-storey elevations
  • Scheduled maintenance programmes

Renovation & Fit-Out

Retail, office and F&B interiors taken from bare unit to opening day, with the branding and signage in the same schedule.

  • Custom carpentry — counters, cabinets, display units
  • Painting & skim coat
  • M&E coordination (electrical, lighting, air-cond)
