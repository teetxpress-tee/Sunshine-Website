Questions

Vehicle wrap questions

How much does a vehicle wrap cost in Malaysia?

It depends on the vehicle size, whether it is a full or partial wrap, and the complexity of the curves and panels. A partial wrap with logo and contact details is the lower-cost option; a full colour-change wrap on a van or lorry costs more. Contact us with your vehicle model for a quote.

How long does a vehicle wrap last?

We print on cast vinyl with a protective laminate for UV resistance. With normal use and hand washing, a quality wrap holds up for several years in Malaysian sun and rain. Lifespan shortens with constant outdoor parking and harsh washing.

Can you wrap lorries and box trucks?

Yes. Large flat panels on cargo bodies and refrigerated trucks are printed as single runs for a seamless finish. We also fabricate and mount illuminated car-top displays.

Will a wrap damage my vehicle paint?

A wrap applied to sound factory paint protects the surface underneath and can be removed later. Wrapping over damaged, resprayed or peeling paint is not recommended, as removal can lift the existing coating.
