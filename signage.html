Questions

Signage questions

How much does a shop signboard cost in Malaysia?

Cost depends on size, material, whether it is illuminated, and the mounting conditions on site. A small non-illuminated fascia sign costs far less than a large halo-lit channel letter set on a high wall. We quote after a site survey so the price includes fabrication and installation.

What is a channel letter sign?

A channel letter is a three-dimensional letter fabricated from aluminium or acrylic with LED lighting inside. Front-lit letters glow through the face; halo-lit or backlit letters throw light onto the wall behind for a softer outline. Both are common for shopfronts and office receptions.

Do you make road signs and traffic signage?

Yes. We fabricate reflective statutory road signage including stop (BERHENTI) signs, chevron hazard markers and directional boards, as well as ceremonial arches.

How long does signage fabrication and installation take?

Lead time depends on the scope and whether local authority approval is needed. A single shopfront sign is much faster than a township monument sign requiring crane installation. We confirm the schedule at quotation stage.
